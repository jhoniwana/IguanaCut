Lossless cutting

Mark the start and end, export without re-encoding. Zero quality loss, no watermark.

📥

Download from 1000+ sites

YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and more via embedded yt-dlp. No external tools needed.

🔗

Share intent

Share a link from any app and the download screen opens with the URL preloaded.

🗂️

Smart exports

Every cut is linked to its source video with auto-generated previews, play and save-to-gallery.

🔒

100% offline & private

Everything runs on your device. Your videos never leave your phone.

🧩

Open source

GPL-2.0 licensed. Audit the code, verify the privacy claims, or fork it.

Install in 3 steps

  1. Download the APK from the latest release.
  2. Open it on your phone and allow install from unknown sources (Android 9+).
  3. Launch IguanaCut, import or download a video, and start cutting.
